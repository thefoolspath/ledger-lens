# Stock Research Center

Status: Proposed for Phase 4; watchlist and notes have a small Phase 2 slice.

## Capabilities

- Search and company overview for held, watched, or ad-hoc securities.
- Period-labelled statements and comparable metrics.
- Locally calculated technical indicators from retained candles.
- Source-linked news, earnings, dividends, splits, and filings when licensed.
- Side-by-side comparison of two to five securities with missing data shown honestly.
- Versioned thesis and journal separating fact, user opinion, and future AI interpretation.

## Provenance

Every external fact stores provider/source URL where available, source period, data-as-of, retrieved-at, currency, units, and normalized-field version. Metrics from different periods or currencies cannot be compared without an explicit normalization warning.

Version 1 watchlists may store target prices, fair-value notes, reasons, risks, and research status, but do not evaluate alerts or issue notifications.
