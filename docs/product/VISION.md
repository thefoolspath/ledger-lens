# Product Vision

LedgerLens gives a personal investor a trustworthy, inspectable record of what was bought or sold, what the portfolio is worth, and why returns changed. It combines an auditable investment ledger, Dime slip reconciliation, market and FX data, and source-linked research without outsourcing canonical calculations to an AI model.

## Outcomes

- Rebuild holdings and cash history from confirmed ledger entries.
- Trace a posted transaction back to its source slip and extraction history.
- View the portfolio in USD and explain the THB result as security-price and FX effects.
- Review data freshness and provider provenance before trusting a quote or metric.
- Preserve an investment thesis and compare it with the eventual outcome.

LedgerLens does not execute trades, provide guaranteed recommendations, or replace broker statements, tax advice, or regulated financial reporting.
